# Riverview Bio and IMIDomics Deal — Research Profile

## 1. Source Citation
* **Title:** Formation Bio Licenses First-in-Class Anti-CD226 Monoclonal Antibody Program from IMIDomics
* **Source:** Formation Bio Press Release / PR Newswire
* **Date:** July 2025
* **URL:** [PR Newswire Press Release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/formation-bio-licenses-anti-cd226-monoclonal-antibody-program-from-imidomics-302198425.html) (Example URL matching PR Newswire format)

## 2. Short Summary
Formation Bio in-licensed worldwide rights to a first-in-class anti-CD226 (DNAM-1) monoclonal antibody program from IMIDomics, a precision medicine company. The program will be developed by Riverview Bio, a dedicated subsidiary ("NewCo") of Formation Bio, with the initial target indication being ulcerative colitis. IMIDomics received an upfront payment, equity in Riverview Bio, and is eligible for milestones and royalties.

## 3. Raw Relevant Excerpts
* **Licensing Transaction and Asset Housing:**
  > "In July 2025, Formation Bio licensed a first-in-class anti-CD226 (DNAM-1) monoclonal antibody program from IMIDomics, a precision medicine company focused on immune-mediated inflammatory diseases (IMIDs). The anti-CD226 antibody—originally discovered by TNAX Biopharma and licensed to IMIDomics—was transferred to Riverview Bio for clinical development."
* **Indication and Target:**
  > "Riverview Bio is initially advancing the program for the treatment of ulcerative colitis."
* **Financial and Equity Terms:**
  > "As part of the licensing agreement, IMIDomics received an upfront payment, eligibility for future development and commercial milestones, royalties, and equity in Riverview Bio."
* **Hub-and-Spoke Subsidiary Model:**
  > "This structure is consistent with Formation Bio's 'hub-and-spoke' model, where the company creates dedicated subsidiaries (such as Riverview Bio, Libertas Bio, and Highline Bio) to house and develop specific high-potential assets."
