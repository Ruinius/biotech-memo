# Formation Bio $372M Series D Financing — Research Profile

## 1. Source Citation
* **Title:** Formation Bio Announces $372 Million Series D Financing
* **Source:** PR Newswire
* **Date:** June 26, 2024
* **URL:** [PR Newswire Press Release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/formation-bio-announces-372-million-series-d-financing-302182745.html)

## 2. Short Summary
Formation Bio (formerly TrialSpark) closed a $372 million Series D financing round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) with significant participation from Sanofi and existing investors. The company will use the capital to aggressively expand its clinical pipeline by acquiring and in-licensing drug candidates and to scale its proprietary AI-native drug development platform.

## 3. Raw Relevant Excerpts
* **Funding and Backing:**
  > "Formation Bio (formerly known as TrialSpark), an AI-native drug developer, today announced it has closed a $372 million Series D financing round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), with significant participation from Sanofi."
* **Use of Capital (In-Licensing & Technology):**
  > "The funding will be used to continue to acquire and in-license candidate drugs, as well as to expand its AI-native drug development platform."
* **Board of Directors Appointments:**
  > "As part of the financing, Scott Kupor, Managing Partner at a16z, and Alfred Lin, Partner at Sequoia, will join the Board of Directors."
* **Participating Investors:**
  > "Additional investors in the round include existing investors Sequoia, Thrive, Emerson Collective, and Lachy Groom, as well as new investors SV Angel Growth and FPV Ventures."
* **Core Philosophy and Model:**
  > "Formation Bio is a tech-driven drug developer that leverages artificial intelligence to streamline and accelerate clinical trials. The company's business model centers on in-licensing or acquiring promising clinical-stage drug assets from other biotechs and pharmaceutical companies, then applying its proprietary AI-native platform to advance them through clinical trials."
