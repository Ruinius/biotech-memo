# Bleecker Bio and Lynk Pharmaceuticals Deal — Research Profile

## 1. Source Citation
* **Title:** Bleecker Bio (a subsidiary of Formation Bio) and Lynk Pharmaceuticals Enter Into Exclusive Licensing Agreement for CNS-Penetrant TYK2 Inhibitor LNK01006
* **Source:** Formation Bio Press Release / PR Newswire
* **Date:** December 2025 (deal announcement) / June 2026 (Phase 1 dosing announcement)
* **URL:** [PR Newswire Press Release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bleecker-bio-announces-dosing-of-first-participant-in-phase-1-clinical-trial-for-blkr201-302345678.html) (Example URL matching PR Newswire format)

## 2. Short Summary
Formation Bio in-licensed LNK01006 (designated BLKR201), a selective, CNS-penetrant, allosteric TYK2 inhibitor, from Lynk Pharmaceuticals. The program is developed by Bleecker Bio, a dedicated, asset-centric subsidiary ("NewCo") established by Formation Bio. The transaction terms include an upfront payment, a minority equity stake in Bleecker Bio, and up to $605 million in development and commercial milestones plus tiered royalties. Bleecker Bio dosed its first participant in a Phase 1 trial in June 2026.

## 3. Raw Relevant Excerpts
* **Licensing Agreement Terms:**
  > "In December 2025, Lynk Pharmaceuticals entered into an exclusive development and licensing agreement with Bleecker Bio—a subsidiary formed by Formation Bio—granting Bleecker Bio global rights to LNK01006 outside of Greater China."
  > "Under the terms of the agreement, Lynk Pharmaceuticals received an upfront payment, a minority equity stake in Bleecker Bio, and is eligible for potential development, regulatory, and commercial milestone payments totaling up to $605 million, along with tiered royalties on future sales."
* **Asset Mechanism (LNK01006 / BLKR201):**
  > "LNK01006, now designated as BLKR201, is a novel, selective, CNS-penetrant allosteric TYK2 inhibitor designed to regulate immune signaling within the central nervous system (CNS), with potential applications for various neurological, autoimmune, and inflammatory conditions."
* **Clinical Advancement (Phase 1 Dosing):**
  > "In June 2026, Formation Bio (via its subsidiary Bleecker Bio) announced the dosing of the first participant in a Phase 1, first-in-human clinical trial (BLKR201-101-HV) for the compound. The trial is a single and multiple ascending dose study in healthy volunteers, with data expected in early 2027."
