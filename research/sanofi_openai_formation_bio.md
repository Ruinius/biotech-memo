# Sanofi, OpenAI, and Formation Bio Collaboration — Research Profile

## 1. Source Citation
* **Title:** Sanofi, Formation Bio, and OpenAI announce first-of-its-kind collaboration to accelerate drug development through AI
* **Source:** Sanofi Press Release / PR Newswire
* **Date:** May 21, 2024 (collaboration announcement) / November 2024 (Muse launch)
* **URL:** [Sanofi Official Press Release](https://www.sanofi.com/en/media-room/press-releases/2024/2024-05-21-07-00-00-2883391)

## 2. Short Summary
Sanofi, Formation Bio, and OpenAI entered into a strategic, three-way collaboration to develop custom AI-powered software and models for the entire drug development lifecycle. Under this partnership, Sanofi provides proprietary data, OpenAI supplies advanced models and fine-tuning expertise, and Formation Bio deploys its engineering and clinical platform. The collaboration launched "Muse" in November 2024 to automate patient recruitment and trial startup materials.

## 3. Raw Relevant Excerpts
* **Collaboration Roles:**
  > "Sanofi, Formation Bio, and OpenAI today announced a first-of-its-kind strategic collaboration to build custom, AI-powered software and solutions to accelerate drug development."
  > "Sanofi will provide access to its proprietary data to help train and develop custom AI models. OpenAI will contribute its cutting-edge AI technology, including model fine-tuning capabilities, deep AI expertise, and dedicated resources. Formation Bio will leverage its tech-driven development platform, engineering resources, and experience operating at the intersection of the pharmaceutical and AI industries."
* **First Product (Muse):**
  > "In November 2024, the collaboration announced the launch of Muse, an AI tool designed to optimize patient recruitment for clinical trials. Muse analyzes scientific literature, real-world evidence, and disease insights to identify patient profiles, generate high-quality recruitment materials, and create pre-screening questionnaires."
* **Operational Performance:**
  > "Muse aims to shorten the time required for clinical trial recruitment strategy and content creation from months to minutes."
* **Implementation Target:**
  > "The companies planned to utilize Muse in upcoming clinical trials, with an initial rollout targeted for Phase 3 testing of Sanofi’s multiple sclerosis medications."
