# Lara Sullivan, M.D. — Research Profile

## Overview

Dr. Lara S. Sullivan is a biotech CEO, enterprise leader, founder, and physician-executive with deep experience spanning drug development, corporate strategy, finance, and business development. She served as President & Chief Executive Officer of Pyxis Oncology from December 2019 until her departure on February 2, 2026.

## Current Status

Dr. Sullivan stepped down as CEO and Chief Medical Officer of Pyxis Oncology effective February 2, 2026. She was succeeded by Thomas (Tom) Civik as Interim CEO while the Board conducts a structured search for a permanent CEO.

**Source:** Pyxis Oncology 8-K Filing, February 6, 2026. [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1782223/000119312526041169/pyxs-20260206.htm) | [StockTitan Summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/PYXS/8-k-pyxis-oncology-inc-reports-material-event-9d8a91976bbe.html)

## Career Timeline

### Pyxis Oncology — CEO & Director (December 2019 – February 2026)

- Appointed CEO and Director on December 11, 2019, succeeding founding CEO David Steinberg
- Led the company's development of next-generation ADCs, including micvotabart pelidotin (MICVO/PYX-201), a first-in-concept EDB+FN-targeted ADC
- Prior to Sullivan's appointment, Pyxis raised a **$22 million Series A** from Leaps by Bayer, Longwood Fund, Agent Capital, and Ipsen
- Closed a **$152 million Series B financing** (March 2021), led by Arix Bioscience and RTW Investments, with participation from Perceptive Advisors, RA Capital Management, Pfizer Ventures, and others — bringing total funding to **$174 million**
- Oversaw Pyxis's **IPO on October 8, 2021** (NASDAQ: PYXS), pricing 10.5 million shares at **$16/share** (top of range), raising **$168 million** in gross proceeds at a post-money valuation of approximately **$524 million** — notable as a preclinical-stage company
- Acquired Apexigen, Inc. in 2023 to expand the antibody pipeline
- Led the company through the COVID-19 pandemic, establishing safety/wellness/business impact as guiding principles
- Advanced MICVO into Phase 1 clinical studies in recurrent/metastatic head and neck squamous cell carcinoma (HNSCC)

### SpringWorks Therapeutics — Founding President (2017–2019)

- Conceived of and executed the clinical-stage spin-out from Pfizer, securing rights to four clinical-stage experimental therapies
- Raised the **$103 million Series A financing** (September 2017), funded by Bain Capital Life Sciences, Bain Capital Double Impact, OrbiMed, Pfizer, and LifeArc — one of the largest Series A rounds in biotech history at the time
- Raised the **$125 million Series B financing** (April 2019), led by Perceptive Advisors with participation from GSK and existing investors, to advance two late-stage rare oncology programs toward regulatory approval
- SpringWorks IPO'd on **September 13, 2019** at **$18/share**, raising **$162 million** in gross proceeds at a post-money valuation of approximately **$754 million** (NASDAQ: SWTX)
- In April 2025, Merck KGaA acquired SpringWorks for **$3.9 billion** ($47/share in cash), representing a 26% premium to the unaffected 20-day VWAP — one of the largest biopharma M&A deals of 2025

## Key Insights for Memo Context

1. **Spin-out expertise:** Sullivan's signature achievement is conceiving and executing the SpringWorks spin-out from Pfizer — a model of extracting value from pharma's shelved assets. SpringWorks went from $103M Series A to $3.9B acquisition by Merck KGaA in under eight years, a ~38x return on the initial investment.
2. **Capital formation track record:** Sullivan raised both the $103M Series A (Bain Capital, OrbiMed, Pfizer) and $125M Series B (Perceptive Advisors, GSK) for SpringWorks, and then led Pyxis through a $152M Series B and $168M IPO at a $524M post-money valuation — all as a preclinical-stage company.
3. **Cross-functional leadership:** Unusual combination of M.D. + M.B.A. + finance experience (McKinsey, PE, equity research) makes her a versatile operator across clinical, strategic, and financial domains.
4. **ADC focus at Pyxis:** Under her leadership, Pyxis developed a novel extracellular-targeting ADC approach (MICVO) with encouraging Phase 1 data in HNSCC.
5. **Relationships-driven career:** Sullivan explicitly attributes her career progression to relationships rather than domain expertise alone.
6. **Departure context:** Her February 2026 departure from Pyxis coincides with the company advancing MICVO through clinical studies, suggesting a potential strategic transition point. Notably, Beeline Medicines (launched April 2026 with a $300M Series A from Bain Capital) is led by CEO Saqib Islam and President/COO Badreddin Edris — both formerly of SpringWorks.
