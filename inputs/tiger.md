About Tiger Huang
Tiger Huang is a hands-on-keyboard AI executive with extensive experience in Forward Deployed AI Engineering, Digital Transformations, and Corporate Strategy. Most recently, Tiger led C3 AI (ticker: AI)'s West Coast Forward Deployed AI Product Management team responsible for working side-by-side with customers to achieve success in building and deploying AI. Tiger's diverse customer portfolio included Tesla, Bloom Energy, Vonage, Quest Diagnostics, and others. Prior to C3 AI, Tiger supported Fortune 500 clients at McKinsey & Company for 10 years from 2009 to 2013 and from 2016 to 2021. His work in Life Sciences included Clinical Trial Optimization using bottom-up simulation, R&D Portfolio Optimization using Monte Carlo modeling, and Personalized Medicine Strategy using scenario analysis. From 2013 to 2016, Tiger co-founded a healthcare SaaS analytics company that served 1M+ patients and two health systems.
